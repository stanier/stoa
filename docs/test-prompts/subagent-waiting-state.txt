This is a multi-turn test of the sub-agent tools. Send each numbered message as a separate turn
and verify the result before proceeding to the next.

--- TURN 1 ---
Spawn a sub-agent whose entire task is: "Immediately call wait_for_message and wait. Do not do
anything else until you receive a message." Save the returned agent_id. Then immediately call
list_agents and report whether the agent's status is "waiting". Do not take any other action.

--- TURN 2 ---
Send the waiting agent (from turn 1) the message: "Your task is now complete. Please summarize
what happened." Then call list_agents and report the agent's current status. Do not take any
other action.

--- TURN 3 ---
Call list_agents and report the final status of the first agent (should be "completed"). Do not
take any other action.

--- TURN 4 ---
Spawn a second sub-agent with the same task: "Immediately call wait_for_message and wait. Do not
do anything else until you receive a message." Then call list_agents and confirm it shows
"waiting". Do not take any other action.

--- TURN 5 ---
Cancel the second agent using cancel_agent. Then call list_agents and confirm it shows
"cancelled". Do not take any other action.
